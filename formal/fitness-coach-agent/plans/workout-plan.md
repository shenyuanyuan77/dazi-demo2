## Progression Rules

- Prioritize stable technique and pain-free range of motion.
- Increase only one variable at a time: repetitions, load, sets, duration, or difficulty.
- Keep most working sets below technical failure unless a qualified coach has prescribed otherwise.
- Reduce volume or intensity when recovery, sleep, illness, or pain indicates a need.

## Adjustment Triggers

Review the plan when:

- The user consistently completes or misses the planned workload.
- Session duration no longer fits the schedule.
- Equipment or training location changes.
- Pain, unusual symptoms, or recovery problems appear.
- The primary goal changes.

## Safety Note

Stop an exercise that causes sharp or worsening pain, dizziness, faintness, chest pain, or unusual breathing difficulty. Seek appropriate professional care when symptoms are severe or persistent.
